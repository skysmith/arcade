# Forest Bible

World handoff for the `Whispering Willow` adventure build.

## Core Party

### Fern

- Penny's character
- elf druid
- thoughtful, magical, nature-connected
- tends toward healing, calming, sensing, and understanding creatures rather than charging first
- has a familiar cat named Frieren

### Frieren

- Fern's cat familiar
- small but important emotional anchor
- can be used for scouting, sensing danger, comic relief, and comfort
- should feel like a real companion, not just a prop

### Jude's Character

- frontline, brave adventurer energy
- protector type
- the one who steps forward when something dangerous needs facing
- strong contrast with Fern's gentler magical approach

## Tone

The world should feel:

- magical but kid-safe
- adventurous with some mystery
- forest fairy-tale more than grimdark fantasy
- dangerous in places, but ultimately about courage, friendship, and mercy
- full of creatures who are not always enemies just because they look scary

Touchstones:

- hidden doors in hills
- old inns
- whispering trees
- magical rivers
- odd creatures who might become friends
- problems caused by fear, imbalance, or misunderstood magic

## Main Premise

Something is wrong in the forest.

The land is out of balance.
Plants, animals, rivers, and moods all feel wrong.
The adventure is not mainly about killing monsters.
It is about understanding what wounded the woods and helping set them right.

## Known Story So Far

### Redwood Inn

- cozy home base
- source of food, rumors, familiar faces, and returns between journeys

### Whispering Willow

- sacred or ancient magical landmark
- introduced by a mysterious hooded figure
- likely tied to the deeper forest truth

### Goblin Trouble

- early visible danger in the woods
- probably not the true cause of the forest sickness
- may be scared, displaced, or reacting to the disturbance

### Crooked Hill-Door

- hidden threshold tracked from the goblins
- could lead to tunnels, old shrine space, root-paths, or forgotten ruins

### Mossjaw

- giant forest boar
- scary at first, but important proof that compassion matters
- should remain a recurring ally, clue-source, and grumpy emotional landmark

### Riverbank Child

- latest major story pivot
- frightened child tied to strange magic at the river
- likely not evil, just overwhelmed and tangled in power larger than them

## Important Locations

- `Redwood Inn`: safe hub, gossip, rest, launch point
- `Whispering Willow`: sacred tree, old memory place, or spirit crossing
- `Crooked Hill-Door`: hidden dungeon-lite threshold
- `Riverbank`: emotionally important scene where the truth begins to surface

## Themes

- friendship over force
- the wild is alive
- fear makes magic worse
- small heroes matter
- mercy reveals the real story

## Likely World Rules

- druid magic is tied to emotion, nature, and balance
- the forest reacts when harmony is broken
- magical places speak through signs, weather, whispers, and animal behavior
- creatures may become dangerous when old magic is disturbed
- some ancient heart, spirit, relic, or wound beneath the woods is fading, blocked, or frightened

## NPC And Creature Roster

- mysterious hooded figure
- Mossjaw
- goblins
- frightened child

## Unanswered Questions

- what exactly is wrong with the forest?
- what is the Whispering Willow?
- what lies beyond the Crooked Hill-Door?
- are the goblins serving something, fleeing something, or just surviving?
- what is the child's connection to the river?
- is there an ancient spirit, relic, wound, or curse beneath all of this?
- who was the hooded figure really?

## One-Sentence Summary

An elf druid, her cat familiar, and her brave companion set out from the Redwood Inn to heal a troubled forest, uncovering goblin tunnels, uneasy creatures, whispering magic, and a frightened child bound to the mystery at the river's edge.
