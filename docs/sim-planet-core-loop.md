# Sim Planet Prototype: Core Loop

Status: design note
Purpose: lock the first playable loop for the orbital civilization prototype.

## Player Goal
Turn an unstable starter star system into a jump-capable industrial hub, then spend the jump charge to seed a new system with better resources and higher hazards.

## Core Playable Loop
1. **Survey the system**: scan the star, planets, moons, asteroid belts, and anomalies to reveal resource deposits, orbital stability, spin rate, habitability, and hazard timers.
2. **Harvest resources**: collect mass and volatiles from planets or asteroids, lift plasma from the star, and gather exotic matter from black-hole or anomaly sites when present.
3. **Build orbital structures**: place extractors, collectors, shipyards, defense grids, and jump infrastructure in stable orbital slots.
4. **Manipulate bodies**: redirect asteroids into chosen targets to alter planet spin, orbital radius, axial tilt, or crust composition; each impact trades immediate disruption for long-term system value.
5. **Stabilize and expand**: use structures and impacts to improve habitability, resource throughput, and orbital safety while keeping the system inside stability limits.
6. **Unlock travel**: complete a jump gate, charge it with plasma and exotic matter, select a surveyed destination, and launch a colony ark.
7. **Jump to the next system**: carry a small seed inventory, one unlocked structure blueprint, and accumulated progression bonuses into a new procedurally generated system.

## Prototype Resources
- **Mass**: bulk material from asteroids, moons, and stripped planets; used for structures and asteroid guidance.
- **Volatiles**: water, gases, and organics; used for biosphere retrofits, population support, and cooling.
- **Plasma**: star-lifted energy matter; used for high-tier power, solar lifting, and jump charging.
- **Exotic Matter**: rare black-hole or anomaly output; used for jump gates, gravity tools, and late-system upgrades.
- **Stability**: system health budget affected by orbital crowding, impacts, radiation, and failed experiments; reaching zero ends the run.

## Prototype Structures
- **Survey Array**: reveals body stats, resource nodes, impact predictions, and jump destinations.
- **Mass Driver**: redirects asteroids and moves mined mass between bodies and orbital worksites.
- **Orbital Foundry**: converts mass and volatiles into structure modules.
- **Solar Lifter**: harvests plasma from the star while increasing flare risk.
- **Habitat Ring**: converts stable orbit and volatiles into population capacity and passive labor.
- **Shield Lattice**: absorbs flare damage, impact fallout, and orbital debris.
- **Jump Gate**: win-condition megastructure that requires plasma, exotic matter, and system stability to activate.

## Win, Fail, And Progression
- **Primary win condition**: activate the Jump Gate and launch a colony ark before system stability reaches zero.
- **Primary fail condition**: system stability reaches zero from unmanaged hazards, failed impacts, over-harvesting, or unshielded stellar events.
- **Soft fail pressure**: core planets can become uninhabitable, asteroid paths can destroy structures, and the star can enter flare escalation if solar lifting is abused.
- **Progression condition**: each successful jump unlocks one durable advantage for later systems, chosen from a structure blueprint, starting resource boost, survey range upgrade, impact-control precision, or exotic-matter efficiency.
- **Session arc**: early systems teach scanning and mining, mid systems require asteroid manipulation and planet retrofitting, and later systems force black-hole harvesting or aggressive solar lifting to power interstellar travel.
